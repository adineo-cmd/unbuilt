How to use the census

  1. Pick a field you can actually work in — not the one with the best video.
  2. Avoid the solid cards. That crowd needs no reinforcements.
  3. Study the outlined cards: something exists but stalls. Finishing it can beat inventing.
  4. Take a dashed card and check it against reality: search for products, message the makers of the closest attempt, then decide.

Judgement calls, definitions and sources are explained in the methodology.
